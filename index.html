Blip

Expense tracker. Log a spend in three seconds.

Privacy policy

Blip collects no data. There is no account, no sign-up, no analytics, no advertising and no server.

  • Everything you enter — amounts, categories, notes, budgets — is stored only on your iPhone.
  • Nothing is sent to the developer or to any third party.
  • Exports (CSV or JSON) are created only when you ask, and go only where you choose to send them.
  • The optional Face ID lock is handled entirely by iOS; Blip never sees your biometric data.
  • The optional daily reminder is a local notification scheduled on your device.

Deleting the app deletes all of its data. If this policy ever changes — for example, if optional iCloud sync is added — this page will be updated before the change ships.

Last updated: 9 October 2026.
